Background

the
 
Study
 
 
The
 
rapid
 
advancement
 
of
 
machine
 
learning
 
and
 
data-driven
 
technologies
 
has
 
significantly
 
influenced
 
the
 
field
 
of
 
education,
 
particularly
 
in
 
understanding
 
and
 
improving
 
student
 
learning
 
behaviors.
 
In
 
recent

educational
 
data
 
mining
 
has
 
enabled
 
the

of
 
various
 
machine
 
learning
 
models
 
to
 
analyze
 
student
 
data,
 
identify
 
behavioral
 
patterns,
 
and
 
predict
 
academic
 
performance.
 
Studies
 
report
 
that
 
machine
 
learning
 
models
 
such
 
as
 
SVM,
 
Decision
 
Trees,
 
and
 
ANN
 
can
 
achieve
 
high
 
accuracy
 
levels,
 
often
 
exceeding
 
85%,
 
depending
 
on
 
the
 
dataset
 
and
 
feature
 
selection.
 
 
Educational
 
data
 
mining
 
has
 
been
 
widely
 
used
 
to
 
process
 
large
 
volumes
 
of
 
academic
 
data
 
and
 
extract
 
meaningful
 
patterns
 
related
 
to
 
student
 
performance.
 
Classification
 
models
 
such
 
as
 
SVM
 
have
 
been
 
reported
 
to
 
achieve
 
accuracy
 
levels
 
of
 
around
 
90%
 
to
 
94%,
 
while
 
Decision
 
Tree
 
models
 
typically
 
achieve
 
approximately
 
85%
 
to
 
92%
 
accuracy
 
in
 
predicting
 
student
 
outcomes
 
(Yağcı,
 
2022).
 
Similarly,
 
Artificial
 
Neural
 
Networks
 
(ANN)
 
have
 
demonstrated
 
accuracy
 
levels
 
exceeding
 
90%
 
due
 
to
 
their
 
ability
 
to
 
model
 
complex
 
nonlinear
 
relationships
 
within
 
student
 
datasets.
 
Despite
 
these
 
strong
 
results,
 
these
 
models
 
primarily
 
focus
 
on
 
prediction
 
and
 
often
 
produce
 
static
 
outputs
 
that
 
do
 
not
 
adapt
 
to
 
changes
 
in
 
student
 
behavior
 
over
 
time.
 
 
 
Study
 
habits
 
are
 
recognized
 
as
 
one
 
of
 
the
 
most
 
significant
 
factors
 
influencing
 
academic
 
success,
 
as
 
they
 
reflect
 
how
 
students
 
manage
 
their
 
 

[PAGE 4]
time,
 
organize

and

in

To

These

In

Classification

such
 
as

and

Trees

accuracy

lack
 
behavioral
 
context,
 
while
 
clustering
 
techniques
 
capture
 
behavioral
 
patterns

cannot
 
predict
 
outcomes.
 
Moreover,

By
